# Allocations (ERC20 framework)

These allocation buckets describe the wrapped MAZZE strategy on Ethereum.

## Allocation framework (reference)
- Public Sale and Liquidity: 52% (2,600,000,000 MAZZE)
- Ecosystem Development Fund: 20% (1,000,000,000 MAZZE)
- Team Growth Fund: 12% (600,000,000 MAZZE)
- Community Engagement Fund: 8% (400,000,000 MAZZE)
- Marketing and Promotion Fund: 8% (400,000,000 MAZZE)

## Current operational note
- Live wrapped MAZZE baseline used by current docs: 4,900,000,000 MAZZE on
  Ethereum.
- The bridge bootstrap reserve is 1,000,000,000 MAZZE.
- That reserve is sourced partially from Ecosystem Development and Team Growth
  funds.

## Public Sale and Liquidity (52%)
The Public Sale and Liquidity bucket is designed to support early market access
and stable market-making.

- Liquidity lock-up period: 12 months on Unicrypt.

## Ecosystem Development Fund (20%)
Supports ecosystem expansion incentives for builders and communities.

- Lock-up period: 1 month cliff, then linear vesting over 11 months.
- Uncx Network: https://etherscan.io/tx/0x9214d6166696c0fd4f11d6ba464337f4a8cabc622a83772f45835cf8276f2ae3

## Team Growth Fund (12%)
